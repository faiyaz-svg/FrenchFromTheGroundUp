# French From the Ground Up

This is a self-study French book for a complete beginner who wants to get to B2: speaking, listening and writing French well enough for a conversation in Montreal, a trip around Quebec, or a bilingual job in Canada. It teaches standard French, and from the first stage it also teaches you to understand Quebec French: the accent, the everyday words and the casual speech.

You study about 30 minutes a day. Each lesson is a short read with audio on every French line, then a 30-minute routine that mixes a new lesson, listening and speaking out loud. The book has 16 stages on four lines, one line for each level (A1 to B2), and each lesson is a station. Finish a lesson and its station fills in on the map below. At B2 you can follow most news and meetings and write a clear email.

Start with [how to use this book](00-start-here/index.md), then follow the lines in order.
