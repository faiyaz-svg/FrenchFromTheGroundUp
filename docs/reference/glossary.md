## 0-9

- **24-hour clock**: a way to tell time with hours from 0 to 23, used in timetables and official texts. ([Telling the time](../03-verbs-and-questions/07-telling-time.md))

## A

- **Abbreviation**: a short form of a word, such as *Mme* for *madame*. ([Filling in forms](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/11-filling-in-forms.md), [Texting to make plans](../03-verbs-and-questions/09-texting-to-make-plans.md))
- **Accent**: a small mark on a letter that changes its sound or tells two words apart. ([How French spelling, sounds and rhythm work](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/01-french-sound-map.md))
- **Acknowledgement of receipt**: *un accusé de réception*, a message that confirms a request arrived. ([Handling a request](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/13-handling-a-request.md))
- **Action item**: a task given to a named person with a date. ([Invitations and follow-ups](../09-work-and-the-future/12-invitations-and-follow-ups.md))
- **Active offer**: offering service in both official languages from the first words, without waiting to be asked. ([Serving the public](../09-work-and-the-future/06-serving-the-public.md))
- **Adjectif verbal**: a participle used as an adjective; it agrees with the noun. ([The gérondif and the present participle](../11-reporting-and-opinions/04-gerondif-and-participe-present.md))
- **Adjective**: a word that describes a person or thing, such as *canadien*. ([Être and introducing yourself](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/10-etre-and-introductions.md), [Adjectives and agreement](../04-people-places-and-directions/01-adjectives-agreement.md))
- **Adverb**: a word that tells how, when or how much, and does not change, such as *heureusement*. ([Listening to a story](../05-the-past-what-happened/10-listening-to-a-story.md), [Adverbs ending in -ment](../06-habits-memories-and-food/07-adverbs-in-ment.md))
- **Affrication**: the hiss that Quebec speakers add to *t* and *d* before /i/ and /y/. ([The Quebec accent and street words](../04-people-places-and-directions/10-quebec-accent-and-street-words.md))
- **Agenda**: the list of topics for a meeting, *un ordre du jour*. ([Office and meeting words](../09-work-and-the-future/07-office-and-meeting-words.md))
- **Agreement**: matching the gender and number of an adjective to its noun. ([Adjectives and agreement](../04-people-places-and-directions/01-adjectives-agreement.md), [The passé composé with être](../05-the-past-what-happened/05-passe-compose-etre.md))
- **Agreement of the participle**: with *avoir*, the past participle takes *-e*, *-s* or *-es* when a direct object stands before the verb. ([Direct object pronouns](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/01-direct-object-pronouns.md))
- **Anglicisme**: a word or use that comes from English. ([Fix the errors, B1](../12-real-montreal/12-fix-the-errors-b1.md))
- **Announcement**: a spoken public message, such as the news of a train at a station. ([Hearing times and plans](../03-verbs-and-questions/11-hearing-times-and-plans.md))
- **Antecedent**: the word that a relative pronoun refers to (*la femme* in *la femme qui parle*). ([The relative pronouns qui and que](../08-comparing-duration-and-settling-in/04-relative-qui-que.md))
- **Antécédent**: the noun that a relative pronoun refers back to, such as *plan* in *le plan que je veux*. ([Ce qui, ce que and ce dont](../12-real-montreal/01-ce-qui-ce-que-ce-dont.md))
- **Article**: a short word before a noun that shows its gender and whether it is a particular thing or any one. ([Gender and articles](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/04-gender-and-articles.md), [A short self-introduction](../04-people-places-and-directions/12-short-self-introduction.md))
- **Auxiliary**: the verb *avoir* or *être* that helps to build a compound tense. ([The plus-que-parfait](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/01-plus-que-parfait.md))
- **Auxiliary verb**: the helper verb in a compound tense, *avoir* or *être*. ([The passé composé with avoir](../05-the-past-what-happened/03-passe-compose-avoir.md))

## B

- **Background**: the situation in which events happen: weather, place, state of mind. ([Imparfait or passé composé](../06-habits-memories-and-food/06-imparfait-vs-passe-compose.md), [Describing your childhood](../06-habits-memories-and-food/11-describing-your-childhood.md))
- **BAGS**: a memory trick for adjectives that go before the noun: beauty, age, goodness, size. ([Adjectives and agreement](../04-people-places-and-directions/01-adjectives-agreement.md))
- **Balado**: the OQLF's recommended term for a podcast in Quebec. ([Music and TV in Quebec](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/09-quebec-music-and-tv.md))
- **Bienséance**: the unwritten rules of what is polite to say, to whom, and where. ([Sacres: recognise, don't use](../12-real-montreal/05-sacres-recognise-dont-use.md))
- **Bulletin de nouvelles**: a short broadcast that gives headlines, reports and the weather. ([Radio news](../11-reporting-and-opinions/10-radio-news.md))

## C

- **Calque**: a phrase copied word for word from another language, such as *bienvenue* used to mean "you're welcome". ([Bonjour-hi and other Montreal greetings](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/09-bonjour-hi-montreal-greetings.md), [A cover letter](../12-real-montreal/10-cover-letter.md), [Fix the errors, B1](../12-real-montreal/12-fix-the-errors-b1.md))
- **Cardinal number**: a number used to count, such as *trois* or *vingt*. ([Numbers from 0 to 100](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/06-numbers-0-100.md))
- **Casual speech**: the way people speak with friends, with shortened forms and fillers, not used in formal writing. ([Casual speech at the resto](../06-habits-memories-and-food/10-casual-speech-at-the-resto.md))
- **Chequing account**: *un compte chèques*, an account for daily payments. ([At the bank](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/08-at-the-bank.md))
- **Chevauchement**: the moment when two people speak at the same time in a conversation. ([A real-speed conversation](../12-real-montreal/07-real-speed-conversation.md))
- **Chronique**: a short opinion text in a newspaper or on the radio. ([Opinion columns](../11-reporting-and-opinions/12-opinion-columns.md))
- **Chunk**: a group of words learned and said as one piece, before you know the grammar inside it. ([Ordering at a café](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/08-ordering-at-a-cafe.md), [Making plans](../03-verbs-and-questions/08-making-plans.md), [At the doctor and the pharmacy](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/07-doctor-and-pharmacy.md), [Agreeing and disagreeing in meetings](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/07-agreeing-in-meetings.md))
- **Citation**: the exact words of a person, put between guillemets in a text. ([A magazine article](../12-real-montreal/11-magazine-article.md))
- **Clause**: a group of words with a verb, which can be a full sentence or part of one. ([Si clauses and hypotheses](../09-work-and-the-future/04-si-clauses-hypotheses.md))
- **Close**: the short ending of a message, such as *Bises* or *À bientôt*. ([A message about yesterday](../05-the-past-what-happened/11-message-about-yesterday.md))
- **Closed syllable**: a syllable that ends in a consonant sound. ([The Quebec accent and street words](../04-people-places-and-directions/10-quebec-accent-and-street-words.md))
- **Closed vowel**: a vowel said with the jaw nearly closed, such as /e/. ([The e vowels](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/04-vowels-e-family.md))
- **Closing**: the polite phrase before your name, such as *Cordialement,*. ([Work email basics](../09-work-and-the-future/10-work-email-basics.md), [A formal request](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/11-formal-request.md))
- **Closing formula**: the polite phrase before your name at the end of an email or a letter. ([A short booking email](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/11-short-booking-email.md))
- **Command (imperative)**: a verb form that tells someone what to do, such as *tournez*. ([Asking for directions](../04-people-places-and-directions/08-asking-directions.md))
- **Comparative**: a form that says more, less or the same amount of a quality (*plus grand que*, *aussi grand que*). ([Comparatives](../08-comparing-duration-and-settling-in/01-comparatives.md))
- **Complément d'agent**: the person or thing that does the action in a passive sentence, introduced by *par* (sometimes *de*). ([The passive voice](../11-reporting-and-opinions/01-passive-voice.md))
- **Concordance des temps**: the rule that changes the tense of the reported words when the reporting verb is in the past. ([Reported speech in the past](../11-reporting-and-opinions/03-reported-speech-past.md))
- **Condition**: what must be true for the result to happen. ([Si clauses and hypotheses](../09-work-and-the-future/04-si-clauses-hypotheses.md))
- **Conditionnel de politesse**: the conditionnel used to make a request or a suggestion more polite, such as *Seriez-vous libre?* ([Role play: making plans](../12-real-montreal/08-role-play-making-plans.md))
- **Conditionnel journalistique**: the conditionnel used in news to say that information is not confirmed. ([Radio news](../11-reporting-and-opinions/10-radio-news.md))
- **Conditionnel présent**: a mood made from the futur simple stem plus the imparfait endings. It softens requests, gives advice and says what would happen. ([The conditionnel for politeness](../09-work-and-the-future/03-conditionnel-politeness.md))
- **Conjugate**: to give the forms of a verb for each subject. ([Être and introducing yourself](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/10-etre-and-introductions.md))
- **Connecteur**: a word or phrase that links two sentences or ideas and shows how they relate (addition, contrast, cause, consequence). ([Connectors for opinions](../11-reporting-and-opinions/05-opinion-connectors.md))
- **Consonant**: a sound made by blocking or squeezing the air in the mouth or throat. ([French consonants](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/01-consonants.md))
- **Contract**: *un contrat*, a written agreement between two parties. ([Banking and admin words](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/12-banking-and-admin-words.md))
- **Contraction**: two words that join into one, such as *à* + *le* = *au*. ([Aller and faire](../03-verbs-and-questions/05-aller-and-faire.md), [Prepositions of place](../04-people-places-and-directions/05-prepositions-of-place.md), [The relative pronoun lequel](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/03-relative-lequel.md))
- **Course**: one part of a meal: *l'entrée*, *le plat*, *le dessert*. ([At the restaurant](../06-habits-memories-and-food/08-at-the-restaurant.md))

## D

- **Dead key**: a key that prints nothing until you press the next letter, which then gets the mark. ([Typing accents on your keyboard](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/02-typing-accents.md))
- **Definite article**: *le*, *la*, *l'* (and *les*): points to a particular thing, like "the". ([Gender and articles](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/04-gender-and-articles.md), [Partitive articles: du, de la, des](../06-habits-memories-and-food/01-partitive-articles.md))
- **Demonstrative**: a word that points to a noun, such as *ce*, *cette*, *ces*. ([This, that, and moi, toi](../04-people-places-and-directions/04-this-that-and-moi-toi.md))
- **Description**: a text that says what a place or person is like, with *être*, *avoir* and *il y a*, and no actions. ([A morning at the café](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/12-a-morning-at-the-cafe.md))
- **Digit**: one of the symbols 0 to 9, *un chiffre*. ([Numbers from 0 to 100](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/06-numbers-0-100.md))
- **Diphtongue**: a vowel that changes quality inside one syllable, such as [au] in *pâte* [paut]. ([Quebec vowels and diphthongs](../12-real-montreal/06-quebec-vowels-and-diphthongs.md))
- **Direct object**: the person or thing that receives the action of a verb, such as *l'escalier* in *il a monté l'escalier*. ([The passé composé with être](../05-the-past-what-happened/05-passe-compose-etre.md), [Direct object pronouns](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/01-direct-object-pronouns.md), [Pronoun order](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/06-pronoun-order.md))
- **Discours direct**: the exact words of a speaker, in quotation marks. ([Reported speech](../11-reporting-and-opinions/02-reported-speech.md))
- **Discours rapporté**: what someone said, told in your own sentence, with *que*, *si* or *de* + infinitive. ([Reported speech](../11-reporting-and-opinions/02-reported-speech.md))
- **Dont**: the relative pronoun that replaces *de* + a noun, or shows possession. ([The relative pronouns dont and où](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/02-relative-dont-and-ou.md))

## E

- **Elision**: the loss of a final vowel before a vowel, shown by an apostrophe: *l'hôtel*. ([Spelling rules and liaison](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/03-spelling-sound-rules-and-liaison.md), [Regular -er verbs](../03-verbs-and-questions/02-regular-er-verbs.md))
- **Emprunt**: a word taken from another language, such as *checker* from English. ([Casual speech in Quebec](../12-real-montreal/03-quebec-casual-speech.md))
- **Enchaînement**: the sliding of a pronounced final consonant onto the vowel of the next word: *il est* /i lɛ/. ([Spelling rules and liaison](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/03-spelling-sound-rules-and-liaison.md))
- **Étiquette de registre**: the short label in a dictionary (such as *fam.*) that tells you how formal or casual a word is. ([Slang: recognise all, use some](../12-real-montreal/04-slang-recognise-all-use-some.md))
- **Expletive ne**: a *ne* with no negative meaning, sometimes written after *craindre que*; it is optional. ([The subjunctive: wishes and feelings](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/05-subjunctive-vouloir-que-emotion.md))
- **Expressive accent**: extra force on a word you want to stress, placed where the speaker chooses. ([Rhythm and intonation](../03-verbs-and-questions/01-rhythm-and-intonation.md))

## F

- **Feminine form**: the form of an adjective used with a feminine noun, the base for adverbs in *-ment*. ([Adverbs ending in -ment](../06-habits-memories-and-food/07-adverbs-in-ment.md))
- **Filler**: a small word such as *euh* or *en fait* that fills a pause or links ideas, with little meaning of its own. ([Listening at real speed](../08-comparing-duration-and-settling-in/11-real-speed-intro.md))
- **Filler word**: a small word such as *ben* or *tsé* that fills a pause. ([Casual speech at the resto](../06-habits-memories-and-food/10-casual-speech-at-the-resto.md))
- **Frame**: a fixed sentence pattern with a few slots to fill, such as *Pour ___, appuyez sur le ___*. ([Phone messages and announcements](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/10-phone-messages-and-announcements.md))
- **Francisation**: the process of making French the common language of an organisation. ([Work culture in Quebec](../09-work-and-the-future/08-quebec-work-culture.md))
- **Futur dans le passé**: the conditionnel used for what was still to come at a past moment. ([Reported speech in the past](../11-reporting-and-opinions/03-reported-speech-past.md))
- **Futur proche**: the near future, made with *aller* and an infinitive. ([The futur proche](../04-people-places-and-directions/06-futur-proche.md), [The futur simple: regular verbs](../09-work-and-the-future/01-futur-simple-regular.md))
- **Futur simple**: the one-word future tense, made from the infinitive plus *`-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont`*. ([The futur simple: regular verbs](../09-work-and-the-future/01-futur-simple-regular.md))
- **Futur simple and conditionnel**: the two tenses used most in this stage (lessons 1 to 3). ([A day at the office](../09-work-and-the-future/13-a-day-at-the-office.md))

## G

- **Gender**: the label masculine or feminine that every French noun carries. ([Gender and articles](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/04-gender-and-articles.md))
- **Gérondif**: *en* + participe présent; it shows an action at the same time as the main verb, or the way it is done. ([The gérondif and the present participle](../11-reporting-and-opinions/04-gerondif-and-participe-present.md))
- **Grille d'évaluation**: a table of criteria with levels, used to rate your own work. ([The B1 checkpoint](../12-real-montreal/13-b1-checkpoint.md))

## H

- **H aspiré**: a silent *h* that blocks elision and liaison. ([Spelling rules and liaison](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/03-spelling-sound-rules-and-liaison.md))
- **H muet**: a silent *h* that lets elision and liaison happen. ([Spelling rules and liaison](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/03-spelling-sound-rules-and-liaison.md))

## I

- **Idiom**: a fixed phrase whose meaning is not the sum of its words, such as *avoir faim*. ([Avoir and il y a](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/07-avoir-and-il-y-a.md))
- **Imparfait**: a past tense for habits, descriptions and states. ([The imparfait](../06-habits-memories-and-food/05-imparfait.md))
- **Imperative**: the verb form used to give a command, advice or an invitation: *Parle! Parlez! Parlons!* ([The imperative](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/04-imperative.md))
- **Impersonal**: a sentence with *il* that has no real person behind it, such as *Il est tard*. ([C'est or il est](../04-people-places-and-directions/07-c-est-vs-il-est.md))
- **Impersonal verb**: a verb used only with *il* that stands for nothing in particular (*il pleut*, *il neige*, *il fait froid*). ([Weather and seasons](../08-comparing-duration-and-settling-in/06-weather-and-seasons.md), [Serving the public](../09-work-and-the-future/06-serving-the-public.md))
- **Indefinite article**: *un*, *une* (and *des*): introduces any one thing, like "a". ([Gender and articles](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/04-gender-and-articles.md), [Partitive articles: du, de la, des](../06-habits-memories-and-food/01-partitive-articles.md))
- **Indicatif**: the mood for facts and for opinions that are stated as true; it is the mood after *il me semble que* and *j'ai l'impression que*. ([Connectors for opinions](../11-reporting-and-opinions/05-opinion-connectors.md), [Giving your opinion](../11-reporting-and-opinions/07-giving-opinions.md))
- **Indirect object**: the person an action is aimed at, linked to the verb by *à*. ([Indirect object pronouns](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/02-indirect-object-pronouns.md), [Pronoun order](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/06-pronoun-order.md))
- **Indirect object pronoun**: a pronoun that replaces *à* + a person: *me*, *te*, *lui*, *nous*, *vous*, *leur*. ([Indirect object pronouns](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/02-indirect-object-pronouns.md))
- **Infinitive**: the dictionary form of a verb, such as *parler* "to speak". ([Regular -er verbs](../03-verbs-and-questions/02-regular-er-verbs.md), [The futur proche](../04-people-places-and-directions/06-futur-proche.md), [Metro maps and signs](../04-people-places-and-directions/09-metro-maps-and-signs.md), [Vouloir, pouvoir and devoir](../06-habits-memories-and-food/02-vouloir-pouvoir-devoir.md))
- **Information question**: a question that begins with a question word. ([Asking questions](../03-verbs-and-questions/04-asking-questions.md))
- **Intonation**: the rise and fall of the voice across a sentence. ([Rhythm and intonation](../03-verbs-and-questions/01-rhythm-and-intonation.md))
- **Invariable**: a word that does not change for gender or number, such as *bon marché*. ([Hearing numbers and prices](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/10-hearing-numbers-and-prices.md), [Colours, sizes and where adjectives go](../04-people-places-and-directions/02-colours-sizes-and-position.md))
- **Inversion**: putting the verb before the pronoun, joined by a hyphen, to make a question. ([Asking questions](../03-verbs-and-questions/04-asking-questions.md), [The passé composé: no, questions and agreement](../05-the-past-what-happened/06-pc-negation-questions-agreement.md), [Phone calls at work](../09-work-and-the-future/05-phone-calls-at-work.md))
- **IPA**: the International Phonetic Alphabet, a set of symbols where each symbol is one sound. ([How French spelling, sounds and rhythm work](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/01-french-sound-map.md))
- **Ironie**: saying the opposite of what you mean, to show what you think. ([Opinion columns](../11-reporting-and-opinions/12-opinion-columns.md))
- **Irregular participle**: a past participle that does not follow the *-é*, *-i*, *-u* rule, such as *fait* or *pris*. ([Irregular past participles](../05-the-past-what-happened/04-irregular-participles.md))
- **Irregular verb**: a verb whose forms do not follow a pattern, so you learn each form. ([Aller and faire](../03-verbs-and-questions/05-aller-and-faire.md), [Regular -ir and -re verbs](../05-the-past-what-happened/01-regular-ir-and-re-verbs.md), [Vouloir, pouvoir and devoir](../06-habits-memories-and-food/02-vouloir-pouvoir-devoir.md), [Savoir, connaître, dire, lire and écrire](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/05-savoir-connaitre-dire-lire-ecrire.md))

## J

- **Juron**: a word said in anger, surprise or pain that is not meant literally. ([Sacres: recognise, don't use](../12-real-montreal/05-sacres-recognise-dont-use.md))

## K

- **Keyboard layout**: the plan that says which character each key makes. ([Typing accents on your keyboard](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/02-typing-accents.md))

## L

- **Landmark**: a building or place that helps you find your way. ([Following directions](../04-people-places-and-directions/11-following-directions.md))
- **Langage familier**: everyday French used among friends, not in formal writing. ([Slang: recognise all, use some](../12-real-montreal/04-slang-recognise-all-use-some.md))
- **Laxing**: making a tense vowel shorter, looser and more open, as Quebec does for /i/, /y/ and /u/ in closed syllables. ([The Quebec accent and street words](../04-people-places-and-directions/10-quebec-accent-and-street-words.md))
- **Letter name**: the word you say for a letter when you spell, such as /ʒe/ for *G*. ([The alphabet and spelling your name](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/07-alphabet-and-spelling-names.md))
- **Lettre de présentation**: a letter that goes with a CV and asks for an interview, also called *lettre d'accompagnement* (the preferred term in the Grand dictionnaire terminologique) or *lettre de motivation*. ([A cover letter](../12-real-montreal/10-cover-letter.md))
- **Liaison**: a silent final consonant that is pronounced before a vowel at the start of the next word: *les amis* /lez ami/. ([Spelling rules and liaison](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/03-spelling-sound-rules-and-liaison.md), [Regular -er verbs](../03-verbs-and-questions/02-regular-er-verbs.md))
- **Ligature**: two letters written as one, such as *œ*. ([Typing accents on your keyboard](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/02-typing-accents.md))
- **Linking word**: a word such as *d'abord* or *ensuite* that connects the steps of a story in order. ([Telling about your weekend](../05-the-past-what-happened/08-telling-about-your-weekend.md))

## M

- **Main idea**: the one point a speaker makes in an answer. ([A radio interview](../10-complex-sentences-and-requests/10-radio-interview.md))
- **Marqueur de discours**: a small word such as *pis*, *ben* or *là* that organises speech more than it adds meaning. ([Casual speech in Quebec](../12-real-montreal/03-quebec-casual-speech.md))
- **Masculine and feminine**: the two grammatical genders of French nouns and adjectives. ([Adjectives and agreement](../04-people-places-and-directions/01-adjectives-agreement.md))
- **Memo**: a short internal document with a sender, a date and a message, *une note de service*. ([Workplace documents](../09-work-and-the-future/11-workplace-documents.md))
- **Métonymie de lieu**: using a place name for its government (*Ottawa annonce*). ([News and institutions in Quebec](../11-reporting-and-opinions/09-quebec-news-and-institutions.md))
- **Minimal pair**: two words that differ in only one sound, such as *tu* and *tout*. ([The vowels a, i, ou and u](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/03-vowels-a-i-ou-u.md))
