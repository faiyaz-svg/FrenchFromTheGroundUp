# Linking words

This page lists the words that join ideas and sentences, grouped by what they do. Use it when you want a better word than *et* or *mais*, or when you need to know which verb form follows a connector.

Lessons for these: connectors for opinions<!-- link: ../11-reporting-and-opinions/05-opinion-connectors.md -->, the subjunctive after conjunctions<!-- link: ../13-subjunctive-and-argument/02-subjunctive-conjunctions.md --> and advanced connectors<!-- link: ../13-subjunctive-and-argument/06-advanced-connectors.md -->.

## How to read the tables

The register column says where the word fits: neutral (used everywhere), formal (writing, reports, speeches) or casual (speech with friends and colleagues). A word with the label "casual" is fine in speech and in a text message, but not in a report or a cover letter.

A **conjunction** (*une conjonction*) is a word that joins two parts of a sentence, and each part has its own verb. Some conjunctions take the **subjunctive** (*le subjonctif*), and others take the **indicative** (*l'indicatif*), the ordinary mood of facts. The Mood column marks this. A dash means the word has no verb after it, or only a noun.

## Adding an idea

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *et* | and | neutral | |
| *aussi* | also | neutral | |
| *de plus* | also, what is more | neutral | |
| *en plus* | on top of that | casual | |
| *d'ailleurs* | besides, by the way | neutral | |
| *en outre* | in addition | formal | |
| *par ailleurs* | also, on another point | formal | |

```fr
Le loyer est cher. De plus, l'appartement est loin du métro. | The rent is high. Also, the apartment is far from the metro.
Il parle français, et il parle aussi espagnol. | He speaks French, and he also speaks Spanish.
En plus, c'est gratuit. | On top of that, it is free.
Le projet est en retard. D'ailleurs, personne ne l'a dit au client. | The project is late. Besides, nobody told the client.
En outre, le contrat prévoit une période d'essai. | In addition, the contract has a trial period.
Par ailleurs, nous avons reçu votre dossier. | Also, we have received your file.
```

## Contrasting two ideas

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *mais* | but | neutral | |
| *pourtant* | yet, even so | neutral | |
| *par contre* | on the other hand | neutral, casual | |
| *cependant* | however | formal | |
| *en revanche* | on the other hand | formal | |
| *alors que* | while, whereas | neutral | indicative |
| *tandis que* | while, whereas | neutral | indicative |

```fr
Je voudrais venir, mais je travaille ce soir. | I would like to come, but I am working tonight.
Il a étudié, pourtant il a échoué à l'examen. | He studied, yet he failed the exam.
Le bus est lent. Par contre, il est bon marché. | The bus is slow. On the other hand, it is cheap.
Les ventes ont baissé. Cependant, les profits sont stables. | Sales have dropped. However, profits are stable.
Le premier plan est coûteux. En revanche, il est rapide. | The first plan is expensive. On the other hand, it is fast.
Elle préfère le café, alors que moi, je préfère le thé. | She prefers coffee, whereas I prefer tea.
Marc travaille le jour, tandis que Sophie travaille la nuit. | Marc works days, while Sophie works nights.
```

## Giving a cause

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *parce que* | because | neutral | indicative |
| *puisque* | since (the reason is known) | neutral | indicative |
| *comme* | since, as (starts the sentence) | neutral | indicative |
| *car* | for, because | formal (writing) | indicative |
| *étant donné que* | given that | formal | indicative |
| *à cause de* | because of (bad or neutral cause) | neutral | noun only |
| *grâce à* | thanks to (good cause) | neutral | noun only |

```fr
Je reste à la maison parce que je suis malade. | I am staying home because I am sick.
Puisque tu y vas, je viens avec toi. | Since you are going, I am coming with you.
Comme il pleut, on prend un taxi. | As it is raining, we are taking a taxi.
Nous devons partir, car la réunion commence à neuf heures. | We have to leave, for the meeting starts at nine.
Étant donné que le budget est limité, nous attendons. | Given that the budget is limited, we are waiting.
Le vol est annulé à cause de la tempête. | The flight is cancelled because of the storm.
J'ai trouvé ce poste grâce à une amie. | I found this job thanks to a friend.
```

## Giving a consequence

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *donc* | so, therefore | neutral | |
| *alors* | so, then | casual | |
| *c'est pourquoi* | that is why | neutral | |
| *par conséquent* | as a result | formal | |
| *si bien que* | so that, with the result that | formal | indicative |

```fr
Il pleut, donc on reste ici. | It is raining, so we are staying here.
Il n'y a plus de lait, alors j'en achète. | There is no more milk, so I am buying some.
Le service était lent, c'est pourquoi nous sommes partis. | The service was slow, which is why we left.
Les prix ont augmenté. Par conséquent, nous réduisons nos achats. | Prices have gone up. As a result, we are cutting our purchases.
Il a oublié le mot de passe, si bien qu'il n'a pas pu se connecter. | He forgot the password, so he could not log in.
```

## Time and order

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *d'abord* | first | neutral | |
| *ensuite*, *puis* | then, next | neutral | |
| *enfin* | finally | neutral | |
| *quand*, *lorsque* | when | neutral, formal | indicative |
| *dès que* | as soon as | neutral | indicative |
| *pendant que* | while | neutral | indicative |
| *après que* | after | neutral | indicative |
| *avant que* | before | neutral | subjunctive |
| *jusqu'à ce que* | until | neutral | subjunctive |

```fr
D'abord, tu coupes les oignons. Ensuite, tu les fais cuire. | First, you cut the onions. Then you cook them.
Enfin, on a trouvé une solution. | Finally, we found a solution.
Appelle-moi dès que tu arrives. | Call me as soon as you arrive.
Je lis pendant que tu cuisines. | I read while you cook.
Nous partons après que le client a signé. | We leave after the client has signed.
Finis ton travail avant que le patron arrive. | Finish your work before the boss arrives.
Reste ici jusqu'à ce que je revienne. | Stay here until I come back.
```

## Giving examples

| Word | Meaning | Register |
|---|---|---|
| *par exemple* | for example | neutral |
| *c'est-à-dire* | that is to say | neutral |
| *comme* | like, such as | neutral |
| *notamment* | in particular | formal |
| *ainsi* | thus, for instance | formal |

```fr
J'aime les fruits, par exemple les pommes et les poires. | I like fruit, for example apples and pears.
On se voit à dix-sept heures, c'est-à-dire après le travail. | We will meet at 17:00, that is, after work.
Il y a des spécialités comme la poutine et la tourtière. | There are specialties such as poutine and tourtière.
Plusieurs villes, notamment Gatineau, offrent ce service. | Several cities, in particular Gatineau, offer this service.
```

## Concession

A **concession** (*une concession*) admits a point and then says the main idea anyway: "although it is cold, we are going out".

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *bien que* | although | neutral, formal | subjunctive |
| *même si* | even if, even though | neutral | indicative |
| *malgré* | despite | neutral | noun only |
| *pourtant* | yet, even so | neutral | |
| *quand même* | all the same | casual | |
| *certes... mais* | admittedly... but | formal | |

```fr
Bien qu'il fasse froid, nous sortons. | Although it is cold, we are going out.
Même s'il pleut, je viens. | Even if it rains, I am coming.
Malgré la pluie, le match a lieu. | Despite the rain, the game goes ahead.
C'est cher, mais je l'achète quand même. | It is expensive, but I am buying it all the same.
Cette idée est certes intéressante, mais elle coûte trop cher. | This idea is admittedly interesting, but it costs too much.
```

## Purpose and conditions

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *pour que* | so that | neutral | subjunctive |
| *afin que* | so that | formal | subjunctive |
| *à moins que* | unless | neutral | subjunctive |
| *sans que* | without (a second subject) | neutral | subjunctive |
| *à condition que* | provided that | neutral | subjunctive |
| *si* | if | neutral | indicative |

```fr
Je répète pour que tu comprennes. | I repeat so that you understand.
Nous écrivons afin que tout le monde soit informé. | We are writing so that everyone is informed.
Je viens, à moins que tu aies besoin de moi ici. | I am coming, unless you need me here.
Il est parti sans que personne le voie. | He left without anyone seeing him.
Je viens à condition que tu conduises. | I am coming provided that you drive.
Si tu veux, on y va. | If you want, we will go.
```

## Concluding

| Word | Meaning | Register |
|---|---|---|
| *finalement* | in the end | neutral |
| *bref* | in short | casual, neutral |
| *en somme* | all in all | neutral |
| *en résumé* | in short, as a summary | formal |
| *en conclusion*, *pour conclure* | to conclude | formal |

```fr
Finalement, nous avons choisi la deuxième offre. | In the end, we chose the second offer.
Bref, je suis arrivé en retard. | In short, I arrived late.
En somme, le projet est réalisable. | All in all, the project is doable.
En résumé, nous proposons trois changements. | As a summary, we propose three changes.
En conclusion, les résultats sont positifs. | In conclusion, the results are positive.
```

## Giving an opinion

| Word | Meaning | Register | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| *à mon avis* | in my opinion | neutral | |
| *selon moi* | in my view | neutral | |
| *d'après lui* | according to him | neutral | |
| *je pense que*, *je trouve que* | I think that, I find that | neutral | indicative |
| *il me semble que* | it seems to me that | neutral | indicative |
| *je ne pense pas que* | I do not think that | neutral | subjunctive |
| *en ce qui me concerne* | as far as I am concerned | formal | |

```fr
À mon avis, c'est une bonne idée. | In my opinion, it is a good idea.
Selon moi, il faut attendre. | In my view, we have to wait.
Je trouve que le film est trop long. | I find that the film is too long.
Il me semble que tu as raison. | It seems to me that you are right.
Je ne pense pas qu'il vienne. | I do not think that he is coming.
En ce qui me concerne, le problème est réglé. | As far as I am concerned, the problem is solved.
```

## Subjunctive or indicative

After these conjunctions, use the subjunctive: *bien que*, *pour que*, *afin que*, *avant que*, *à moins que*, *sans que*, *à condition que*, *jusqu'à ce que*. After these, use the indicative: *parce que*, *puisque*, *pendant que*, *alors que*, *même si*, *dès que*, *quand*.

*Après que* takes the indicative in standard usage, even though you will often hear the subjunctive. The OQLF lists it with the indicative and says the subjunctive here is criticized, because the action is known to have happened. In writing for work or school, write *après qu'il a fini*, not *après qu'il ait fini*. Sources: [OQLF, Banque de dépannage linguistique, subordonnants composés suivis du subjonctif ou de l'indicatif](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/bdl/gabarit_bdl.asp?id=4238&t1=1) and [OQLF, mode verbal avec après que et avant que](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/21132/la-grammaire/le-verbe/modes/concordance-des-temps/mode-verbal-avec-apres-que-et-avant-que), checked 2026. The OQLF table does not list *parce que*, *puisque*, *pendant que*, *alors que*, *même si*, *dès que* or *quand*; they are normally followed by the indicative.
```fr
Après qu'il a fini, nous sommes partis. | After he finished, we left.
Avant qu'il parte, je veux lui parler. | Before he leaves, I want to talk to him.
```
