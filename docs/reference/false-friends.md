# False friends

French words that look like an English word but mean something else, with the French you want for the English meaning. The last section covers anglicisms that the Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF) advises against in Quebec, as of 2026. Look at the table first, then say the example out loud.

## At A1 and A2

| French | Means | Looks like | French for that English word |
|---|---|---|---|
| *actuellement* | at the moment | actually | *en fait*, *vraiment* |
| *attendre* | to wait for | to attend | *assister à* |
| *assister à* | to attend | to assist | *aider* |
| *la librairie* | bookstore | library | *la bibliothèque* |
| *la location* | rental | location | *l'endroit* (m.), *l'emplacement* (m.) |
| *rester* | to stay | to rest | *se reposer* |
| *le coin* | corner | coin | *la pièce de monnaie* |
| *la journée* | the whole day | `journey` | *le voyage*, *le trajet* |
| *la monnaie* | change (coins); currency | money | *l'argent* (m.) |
| *sympathique* | nice, likeable | sympathetic | *compatissant* |
| *demander* | to ask | to demand | *exiger* |
| *rentrer* | to go back in, to go home | to enter | *entrer* |
| *la chance* | luck | chance | *l'occasion* (f.), *la possibilité* |
| *blesser* | to hurt, to injure | to bless | *bénir* |
| *crier* | to shout | to cry | *pleurer* |
| *la cave* | cellar | cave | *la grotte* |
| *passer un examen* | to take an exam | to pass an exam | *réussir un examen* |

```fr
Actuellement, je travaille à Toronto. | At the moment, I work in Toronto.
Il attend le métro depuis dix minutes. | He has been waiting for the metro for ten minutes.
Elle assiste à une réunion demain. | She is attending a meeting tomorrow.
Nous cherchons une librairie près d'ici. | We are looking for a bookstore near here.
Nous cherchons la bibliothèque municipale. | We are looking for the public library.
La location de la voiture coûte 60 $ par jour. | The car rental costs 60 $ a day.
Je reste à l'hôtel jusqu'à samedi. | I am staying at the hotel until Saturday.
Tourne à gauche au coin de la rue. | Turn left at the corner of the street.
Le voyage dure trois heures, mais la journée est belle. | The trip takes three hours, but the day is nice.
Vous avez de la monnaie pour dix dollars? | Do you have change for ten dollars?
Ton collègue est très sympathique. | Your colleague is very nice.
Je te demande de m'aider. | I am asking you to help me.
Il est tard, je rentre à la maison. | It is late, I am going home.
Tu as de la chance, il reste une place. | You are lucky, one seat is left.
Vous aurez l'occasion de visiter Québec. | You will have the chance to visit Quebec City.
Ne crie pas, je t'entends. | Do not shout, I can hear you.
Le vin est dans la cave. | The wine is in the cellar.
Elle a réussi l'examen. | She passed the exam.
Il a mal à la jambe : il s'est blessé au hockey. | His leg hurts: he hurt himself playing hockey.
```

## At B1 and B2

| French | Means | Looks like | French for that English word |
|---|---|---|---|
| *éventuellement* | possibly, if need be | eventually | *finalement*, *à la longue* |
| *sensible* | sensitive | sensible | *raisonnable*, *sensé* |
| *formidable* | great, wonderful | formidable | *redoutable*, *impressionnant* |
| *la déception* | disappointment | deception | *la tromperie* |
| *avoir envie de* | to feel like, to want | envy | *la jalousie* (envy), *envier* (to envy) |
| *le préservatif* | condom | preservative | *le conservateur* |
| *le délai* | time limit, deadline | delay | *le retard* |
| *la conférence* | talk, lecture | conference | *le congrès*, *la réunion* (a work meeting) |
| *prétendre* | to claim | to pretend | *faire semblant* |
| *supporter* | to put up with | to support | *soutenir*, *appuyer* |
| *ignorer* | not to know | to ignore | *ne pas tenir compte de* |
| *large* | wide | large | *grand*, *gros* |
| *la lecture* | reading | lecture | *le cours magistral*, *la conférence* |
| *le résumé* | summary | résumé (CV) | *le curriculum vitæ*, *le CV* |

```fr
Je peux éventuellement venir demain, si j'ai fini mon travail. | I can possibly come tomorrow, if I have finished my work.
Il a finalement réussi. | In the end, he succeeded.
Ma sœur est très sensible. | My sister is very sensitive.
Ton idée est raisonnable. | Your idea is sensible.
Quelle soirée formidable! | What a great evening!
Son adversaire est redoutable. | Their opponent is formidable.
Quelle déception, le concert est annulé. | What a disappointment, the concert is cancelled.
J'ai envie d'un café. | I feel like a coffee.
Il a de la jalousie envers son frère. | He feels envy toward his brother.
Le délai est de trois semaines. | The time limit is three weeks.
Le projet a pris du retard. | The project is running late.
Elle prétend qu'elle ne savait rien. | She claims she knew nothing.
Il fait semblant de dormir. | He is pretending to sleep.
Je ne supporte pas le bruit. | I cannot stand the noise.
La table est large de deux mètres. | The table is two metres wide.
Envoyez votre CV et un résumé de votre expérience. | Send your CV and a summary of your experience.
```

The word *préservatif* is a medical word. Never use it for food. For jam or food additives, use *conservateur*.

## Quebec anglicisms to recognise

These are common in speech in Quebec, and the OQLF Banque de dépannage linguistique advises against them in careful or written French. They are not mistakes of the speaker; they are forms the OQLF advises replacing. You will hear them. Say the standard form. Lines marked as casual are what you may hear.

| You may hear | Prefer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *appliquer* (sur un poste) | *postuler*, *poser sa candidature* | [OQLF on *appliquer*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/21048/les-emprunts-a-langlais/emprunts-semantiques/emploi-deconseille-des-emprunts-appliquer-et-faire-application), [OQLF on *postuler*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/fiche-gdt/fiche/2068642/postuler) |
| *canceller* | *annuler* | [OQLF on *canceller*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/20990/les-emprunts-a-langlais/emprunts-hybrides/les-emprunts-deconseilles-canceller-et-cancellation) |
| *céduler* | *fixer*, *prévoir*, *programmer*, *inscrire à l'horaire* | [OQLF on *céduler*](http://bdl.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/bdl/gabarit_bdl.asp?id=2412) |
| *être en charge de* | *être responsable de*, *être chargé de*, *avoir la responsabilité de* | [OQLF on *être en charge de*](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/20844/les-emprunts-a-langlais/emprunts-syntaxiques/les-emprunts-deconseilles-etre-en-charge-de-et-prendre-charge-de) |
| *définitivement* (as "definitely") | *assurément*, *certainement*, *absolument*, *sans aucun doute* | [OQLF on *définitivement*](https://bdl.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/bdl/gabarit_bdl.asp?id=1102) |

The OQLF notes that *cédule* as a schedule is also an anglicism, but *cédule* stays correct in law and finance (for example, *cédule hypothécaire*). It also notes that *définitivement* is correct in its own meaning, "for good" (*Il a quitté le pays définitivement*).

```fr
J'ai posé ma candidature pour ce poste. | I applied for this job.
Je vais postuler pour ce poste demain. | I will apply for this job tomorrow.
Je vais appliquer sur ce poste. {qc} | I am going to apply for this job.
La réunion est annulée. | The meeting is cancelled.
La réunion est cancellée. {qc} | The meeting is cancelled.
Je vais fixer un rendez-vous avec vous. | I will set an appointment with you.
Je vais céduler un rendez-vous. {qc} | I will schedule an appointment.
Elle est responsable du dossier. | She is in charge of the file.
Elle est en charge du dossier. {qc} | She is in charge of the file.
Vous venez à la fête? Assurément! | Are you coming to the party? Definitely!
Vous venez à la fête? Définitivement! {qc} | Are you coming to the party? Definitely!
```

I left out other anglicisms I could not check against the OQLF pages.
