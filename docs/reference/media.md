# Media

Free French to listen to, watch and look things up in, sorted by level. Everything here is a link to the source. The book does not copy or host any of it. Links were checked in November 2026 (as of 2026). Catalogues change, so check the access and price on each page.

"Canada only" means the source limits its content to viewers in Canada. Toronto is fine.

## Listening and viewing by level

| Source | Accent | Level | Cost and access |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Mauril](https://mauril.ca/fr/) ([web app](https://app.mauril.ca/)) | Quebec and Canadian | A1 to B2, in 8 levels | Free. Canada only |
| [Francolab](https://www.tv5plus.ca/francolab/collections/tous-les-contenus) | Quebec, Ontario and Canadian | A1 to B2, filterable | Check the page for price and access |
| [ICI OHdio](https://ici.radio-canada.ca/ohdio) | Quebec | B1 to B2 | Free, plays without logging in. Some sponsored content. Not made for learners |
| [La journée (est encore jeune)](https://ici.radio-canada.ca/premiere/balados/9887/la-journee-est-encore-jeune) | Quebec | B1 to B2 | Free. A conversational culture podcast with humour |
| [ICI TOU.TV](https://ici.tou.tv/) | Quebec | A2 to B2 | Some titles are free. Many are paid or need a free account. Canada only |
| [Télé-Québec](https://telequebec.tv/) | Quebec | A2 to B2 | Free documentaries and magazine shows. A free account is optional |
| [ONF (National Film Board)](https://www.onf.ca/films/) | Quebec and Canadian | A2 to B2 | Free films. Some titles are limited to Canada and the United States. Choose the original French version |
| [TFO](https://www.tfo.org/) | Franco-Ontarian | A2 to B2 | Check the page for price and access |
| [Idéllo](https://www.idello.org/) | Franco-Ontarian | A2 to B2 | Some titles need a subscription |

Two lists of more sources: the [Quebec government list of Quebec media](https://www.quebec.ca/education/apprendre-le-francais/outils-ressources/medias-et-produits-culturels-quebecois-de-langue-francaise) and the [McGill Learn French resources](https://www.mcgill.ca/learnfrench/resources).

Check in your browser (these block automated checks, so their content was not read): TV5MONDE Apprendre (apprendre.tv5monde.com), RFI "Journal en français facile" (rfi.fr), Savoir média (savoir.media).

## What to use at each level

| Level | Stages | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 1 to 4 | Mauril Débutant 1 and 2, Francolab titles tagged Débutant, the Quebec government and McGill lists |
| A2 | 5 to 8 | Mauril Débutant 3 and 4, Francolab Intermédiaire titles, Télé-Québec, ONF films |
| B1 | 9 to 12 | Mauril Intermédiaire, OHdio podcasts, Télé-Québec, free TOU.TV titles |
| B2 | 13 to 16 | Mauril Avancé, OHdio, Télé-Québec, TFO and Idéllo, ONF documentaries |

Each stage's index page has its own shorter list under "Listen and watch at this level".

## Free reference tools

| Tool | Cost and notes |
|---|---|
| [OQLF Banque de dépannage linguistique](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/) | Free. The Quebec language office's guide to usage, spacing and email greetings |
| [Grand dictionnaire terminologique](https://gdt.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/) | Free. Terms with domain labels, Quebec terminology |
| [Wiktionnaire](https://fr.wiktionary.org/) | Free and open. Conjugation tables, pronunciation audio. Check Quebec words against the OQLF |
| [Le Conjugueur](https://leconjugueur.lefigaro.fr/) | Free, with ads. Standard conjugation, from France |
| [Linguee](https://www.linguee.com/) | Free. Real bilingual examples. Its privacy terms were not read, so don't paste confidential work text |
| [DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/en/translator) | A free tier and paid plans exist. Text you type goes to its servers, so read its privacy statement first and don't paste confidential work text |
| [Usito](https://usito.usherbrooke.ca/) | A dictionary of French as used in Quebec. Access may need a subscription, so check your library or university |

Check in your browser (blocked to automated checks): the [Termium Plus](https://www.btb.termiumplus.gc.ca/tpv2alpha/alpha-fra.html?lang=fra) terminology bank and the [Language Portal of Canada](https://nos-langues.canada.ca/fr), both from the Government of Canada.
