# Say it

You see a sentence. Press the microphone, say it, and see which words the browser heard.

This works in Chrome and Edge, and in recent Safari. Firefox does not support it. Chrome may recognise speech on your device. Otherwise the audio goes to Google or Microsoft to be turned into text.

The browser asks for microphone access the first time.
