# Practice

These drills train one skill each. Pick a drill, choose the highest stage you have finished, and go.

- [Conjugation](conjugation.md): type the verb form.
- [Le or la](le-or-la.md): pick the gender of a noun.
- [Fill in the blank](fill-in-the-blank.md): finish a sentence.
- [Dictation](dictation.md): listen, then type.
- [Numbers](numbers.md): hear a number, price, phone number, time or year, then type the digits.
- [Quebec words](quebec-words.md): match a Quebec word to standard French.
- [Say it](say-it.md): say a sentence and see which words were heard.

## How the drills work

Each card comes from a queue. Cards you miss come back sooner. Cards you get right come back less often.

Typed answers have three results. **Right** means an exact match. **Almost** means the letters are right but an accent or a capital is wrong. The answer is shown and the card comes back. **Wrong** means something else.

Your progress stays in this browser. It is not sent anywhere. Clearing site data erases it.
