## Cheat sheet

Sounds. The consonants have no puff of air on /p t k/, the tongue is on the teeth for /t d/, and /l/ is light. The French /ʁ/ is made at the back of the throat.

| Sound | Spellings | Example |
|---|---|---|
| /k/ | c before a, o, u; qu; k | *café* /kafe/ |
| /s/ | c before e, i; ç; ss; s at the start | *merci* /mɛʁsi/ |
| /g/ | g before a, o, u; gu | *garçon* /gaʁsɔ̃/ (the *ç* is /s/) |
| /ʒ/ | j; g before e, i | *jour* /ʒuʁ/ |
| /ʃ/ | ch | *chat* /ʃa/ |
| /z/ | s between vowels; z | *chose* /ʃoz/ |
| /f/ | f, ph | *photo* /fɔto/ |
| /ɲ/ | gn | *signe* /siɲ/ |
| /ʁ/ | r, rr | *rue* /ʁy/ |
| /j/ | y, i + vowel, ill | *bien* /bjɛ̃/, *fille* /fij/ |
| /w/ | ou + vowel, oi | *oui* /wi/, *moi* /mwa/ |
| /ɥ/ | u + vowel, ui | *huit* /ɥit/, *nuit* /nɥi/ |

Final consonants are usually silent, except often c, r, f, l (CaReFuL): *sac*, *bonjour*, *neuf*, *hôtel*. Liaison is required after a small word before a noun (*les amis* /lez ami/), between pronoun and verb (*vous avez* /vuz ave/) and after short prepositions (*en été*). It is forbidden after *et* and before an h aspiré (*les héros*).

Articles.

| | Masculine | Feminine | Before a vowel | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the | *le* | *la* | *l'* | *les* |
| a, some | *un* | *une* | *un*, *une* | *des* |

Gender hints, "usually": *-tion* is feminine; *-ment*, *-age* and *-eur* are masculine; exceptions include *la peur*, *un problème*. Plurals: add -s; *-eau* and *-eu* take -x; *-al* becomes *-aux*; *-s*, *-x* and *-z* do not change; *un œil* becomes *des yeux*.

Avoir, present, and il y a.

| Pronoun | Form |
|---|---|
| *je* | *ai* (*j'ai*) |
| *tu* | *as* |
| *il / elle* | *a* |
| *nous* | *avons* |
| *vous* | *avez* |
| *ils / elles* | *ont* |

*Il y a* means "there is" and "there are". Age and feelings use *avoir*: *j'ai vingt ans*, *j'ai faim*, *j'ai froid*.

Numbers.

| 0 to 10 | 11 to 16 | Tens | 70 to 99 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *zéro*, *un*, *deux*, *trois*, *quatre*, *cinq*, *six*, *sept*, *huit*, *neuf*, *dix* | *onze*, *douze*, *treize*, *quatorze*, *quinze*, *seize* | *vingt*, *trente*, *quarante*, *cinquante*, *soixante* | *soixante-dix*, *soixante et onze*, *quatre-vingts*, *quatre-vingt-un*, *quatre-vingt-dix* |

*Et un* appears in 21, 31, 41, 51, 61 and 71. Prices: 4,50 $ is *quatre dollars cinquante*.

Key phrases.

```fr
Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît. | I would like a coffee, please.
Pour ici ou pour emporter? Sur place. Pour emporter. | For here or to take away? For here. To take away.
C'est combien? Ça fait six dollars cinquante. | How much is it? That comes to six dollars fifty.
C'est tout. | That's all.
J'ai vingt ans. J'ai faim. J'ai froid. | I'm twenty. I'm hungry. I'm cold.
Il y a une table et deux chaises. | There is a table and two chairs.
Votre nom? Votre prénom? Votre adresse? | Your family name? Your first name? Your address?
Un dépanneur, une liqueur, un courriel, le souper. | A corner shop, a soft drink, an email, the evening meal (Quebec).
```

Forms. Dates are numbers only: 14/03/1998 (JJ/MM/AAAA) or 1998-03-14. An address reads *45, rue Laval, app. 4*, then city, province and postal code.
