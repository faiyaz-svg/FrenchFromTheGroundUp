# Set up voices and audio

The site does not ship recordings. It reads French aloud with the voices in your browser, which come from your computer or your phone. Spend 10 minutes on this page before lesson 1, because good audio matters more than anything else in the first stages.

## The best option: Microsoft Edge

Edge offers online voices that sound natural, including Quebec ones such as "Microsoft Sylvie Online (Natural) - French (Canada)". They need an internet connection. If your computer is managed by an employer or a school, an administrator can switch these voices off.

1. Open the book in Microsoft Edge.
2. Click the Voice control in the page header.
3. Set Accent to Quebec and press Test voice.

If you hear a French voice say a short greeting, you are done with this part. A voice named "Online (Natural)" is the one to keep.

## Chrome, Anki and Windows voices

Chrome and Anki on Windows use the voices installed in Windows, not Edge's online ones. A new Windows computer often has English voices only, so you may hear an English voice try to read French, or nothing at all. Add a French (Canada) voice:

1. Open Windows Settings.
2. Go to Time & language, then Speech, then Manage voices, then Add voices.
3. Search for French (Canada) and add it.

Microsoft changes these labels between Windows builds. If you can't find them, type "speech" or "voices" in the Settings search box and open the result that mentions voices. After adding a voice, close and reopen the browser.

## Choose Quebec or France, and slow speed

The Voice control in the page header has three settings:

- Accent: Quebec or France. The book uses Quebec first, so start there. Switch to France now and then to hear the other accent.
- Speed: Normal or Slow. Use Slow for new sounds and Normal for shadowing.
- Voice: Automatic, or a voice you pick from the list of French voices your browser found.

Press Test voice after each change. The setting is saved in your browser.

## If you hear nothing, or an English voice

- Check that the page tab is not muted and the volume is up.
- Open the Voice control. If the Voice list is empty or has no French voice, add one as above, then reload the page.
- If the list shows a French voice but you hear English, pick that voice by name instead of Automatic.
- Some browsers load voices a few seconds late. Reload and wait 5 seconds before pressing play.
- In Firefox or Safari, voices vary. If it does not work, use Edge on Windows.

## Phones

Android and iPhone both have French voices in their text-to-speech settings. On Android, look under the speech or text-to-speech output settings and install French (Canada) if it is listed. On iPhone, look under Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, then French. Names and paths vary by version, so use the Settings search if you can't find them.

## The Say it tool

The Say it mic button checks what you said by speech recognition. It works in Chrome and Edge only, not in Firefox or Safari.

Speech recognition may run on your device or on a server. As of 2026, Chrome can recognise speech on the device once it has downloaded a language pack, and otherwise it sends the audio to Google. Edge sends the audio to Microsoft unless on-device recognition is on for your version. Read the browser's privacy notice if that matters to you, and don't say anything private into the mic.

The first time you press the mic, the browser asks for permission to use it. Choose Allow. If you chose Block by mistake, click the lock or tune icon in the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, and reload.
