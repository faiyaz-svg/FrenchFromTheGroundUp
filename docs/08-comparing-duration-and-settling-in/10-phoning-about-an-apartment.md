# Phoning about an apartment

<span class="tag tag-speak">Speak</span> <span class="tag tag-a2">A2</span>

After this lesson you can phone about an apartment ad, ask if it is free, what the rent is, how many rooms it has, what is included, whether pets are allowed, and ask for a visit, all with polite *vous* forms.

Before this: [Housing words](09-housing-words.md), [Asking questions](../03-verbs-and-questions/04-asking-questions.md), [Vouloir, pouvoir and devoir](../06-habits-memories-and-food/02-vouloir-pouvoir-devoir.md), [The imperative](../07-pronouns-and-getting-things-done/04-imperative.md). You need: nothing.

## The idea

A phone call about an ad has five steps, and the order is almost always the same. You use *vous* with the owner, because you do not know each other.

### Step 1: greet and give your reason

*Bonjour, madame.* *Je vous appelle pour l'appartement.* ("I'm calling about the apartment.") If you disturb the person, add *Excusez-moi de vous déranger* ("Sorry to bother you"; *déranger* means "to disturb"). Name the ad: *le 4 1/2 à louer à Verdun*. *À louer* means "for rent". The opposite is *à vendre*, "for sale", so keep them apart.

### Step 2: is it free?

*Il est encore libre?* ("Is it still available?") The answer often gives a date: *Il est libre à partir du 1er juillet.* *À partir de* means "starting from" and works with dates and with money: *à partir de 900 $ par mois*.

### Step 3: ask about the details

Ask short questions you already know, and stay polite. Start with *Je voudrais savoir* ("I would like to know") or *Est-ce que* and a calm tone.

| You want to know | You say |
|---|---|
| the rent | *C'est combien par mois?* |
| the rooms | *Combien de pièces y a-t-il?* |
| the heating and lights | *Est-ce que c'est chauffé et éclairé?* |
| what is included | *L'eau chaude est incluse?* |
| pets | *Les animaux sont acceptés?* |

*Une pièce* is a room, and *inclus* (*incluse*) means "included". It agrees like an adjective: *l'eau chaude est incluse*, *le chauffage est inclus*. *Chauffé et éclairé* is the pair you know from [Housing words](09-housing-words.md). Listen for the answer, because *chauffé, oui* and *éclairé, non* is a common reply. *Un espace* is a space: *Il y a beaucoup d'espace.*

### Step 4: ask to visit

*Je peux visiter l'appartement samedi?* or, with the pronoun, *Je peux le visiter samedi?* Better still, use the polite *je voudrais*: *Je voudrais visiter l'appartement.* Say your thanks and repeat the time: *Samedi à dix heures, parfait. Merci, madame.* *Je veux visiter* sounds blunt, like a demand.

### Step 5: show who you are

An owner may ask about you. *Un emploi* is a job, *un revenu* is an income and *une référence* is a reference (a person or a letter that speaks for you). You can say *J'ai un bon emploi* or *Je suis infirmière depuis quatre ans.* If you are asked for papers, say *Bien sûr*, and ask what to bring. The verb *intéresser* means "to interest": *Le logement m'intéresse beaucoup* ("The apartment interests me a lot").

This book does not explain the rules for renters. If you are not sure what an owner may ask for, read the TAL pages (tal.gouv.qc.ca) before the call.

## Hear it

Tanya is 27 and works as a nurse. She phones Mme Bouchard, 62, who owns a 4 1/2 in Verdun. They use *vous*. Listen for the five steps: greeting, availability, details, visit and thanks.

```fr
Bouchard: Allô? | Hello?
Tanya: Bonjour, madame. Excusez-moi de vous déranger. Je vous appelle pour le 4 1/2 à louer à Verdun. | Hello, madam. Sorry to bother you. I'm calling about the 4 1/2 for rent in Verdun.
Bouchard: Oui, bonjour. Il est libre à partir du premier juillet. | Yes, hello. It's free from July 1st.
Tanya: C'est combien par mois, s'il vous plaît? | How much is it a month, please?
Bouchard: Mille cinquante dollars par mois. | One thousand fifty dollars a month.
Tanya: Est-ce que c'est chauffé et éclairé? | Is it heated, with lights included?
Bouchard: Chauffé, oui. Éclairé, non. L'eau chaude est incluse. | Heated, yes. Lights, no. The hot water is included.
Tanya: Combien de pièces y a-t-il? | How many rooms are there?
Bouchard: Il y a quatre pièces : un salon, une cuisine et deux chambres. Il y a aussi un balcon. | There are four rooms: a living room, a kitchen and two bedrooms. There is also a balcony.
Tanya: Les animaux sont acceptés? J'ai un chat. | Are pets allowed? I have a cat.
Bouchard: Un chat, c'est correct. | A cat is fine.
Tanya: Je voudrais visiter l'appartement. Je suis libre samedi. | I'd like to visit the apartment. I'm free on Saturday.
Bouchard: Samedi à dix heures, c'est possible. Vous avez un emploi? | Saturday at ten is possible. Do you have a job?
Tanya: Oui, je suis infirmière depuis quatre ans. Le logement m'intéresse beaucoup. | Yes, I've been a nurse for four years. The apartment interests me a lot.
Bouchard: Parfait. Apportez une référence, s'il vous plaît. | Perfect. Bring a reference, please.
Tanya: Bien sûr. Merci, madame. À samedi! | Of course. Thank you, madam. See you Saturday!
```

## Try it

1. Put the five steps in order: ask to visit, give your reason, ask about details, say thanks, ask if it is free.
2. Write the question you would use for each of these: the rent, the heating, pets, a visit.
3. Choose. *Un appartement ___ (à louer / à vendre) est pour un locataire.* *L'eau est ___ (inclus / incluse).* *Il est libre ___ partir du 1er juillet.* (*à* or *de*)
4. Fix the mistake in each line.

```fr
Je veux visiter l'appartement. {x}
Le logement intéresse moi. {x}
Tu es libre le 4 1/2? {x}
```

5. Do the call. Play the part of Tanya out loud with a recording of Mme Bouchard's lines, or with a partner. Use your own name and job. Stay at normal speed.

1. Give your reason, ask if it is free, ask about details, ask to visit, say thanks.
2. *C'est combien par mois?* *Est-ce que c'est chauffé?* *Les animaux sont acceptés?* *Je voudrais visiter l'appartement.*
3. *à louer*, *incluse* (*l'eau* is feminine), *à*.
4. *Je voudrais visiter l'appartement.* *Le logement m'intéresse.* *Le 4 1/2 est encore libre?* (*libre* describes the apartment, not you.)
5. Check that you used *vous*, *je voudrais* for the visit, and *merci* at the end.

## Common mistakes

Using *tu* with the owner. Use *vous*, *je vous appelle* and *s'il vous plaît*.

Saying *Je veux visiter*. Use *Je voudrais visiter*, which is polite.

Mixing *à louer* and *à vendre*. An apartment for rent is *à louer*.

## In Quebec

You will see *chauffé* and *éclairé* in Quebec ads (see [Housing words](09-housing-words.md)). They are two separate items, so ask about each one and listen to both answers: *chauffé* does not guarantee *éclairé*.
