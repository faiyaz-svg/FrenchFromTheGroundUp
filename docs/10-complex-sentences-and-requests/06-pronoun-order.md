## L'idée

You can replace one noun with one pronoun: *Je donne le dossier à Marc* becomes *Je le donne à Marc*, or *Je lui donne le dossier*. When both the thing and the person are replaced, two pronouns stand before the verb: *Je le lui donne.* ("I give it to him.") This lesson gives you the order.

### The order before the verb

Read the table from left to right. Take one pronoun from the columns, in this order. You never take two from the same column.

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *me, te, se, nous, vous* | *le, la, l', les* | *lui, leur* | *y* | *en* |

```fr
Il me le donne. | He gives it to me. (column 1, then 2)
Je te les envoie. | I send them to you. (1, 2)
Je le lui donne. | I give it to him or her. (2, then 3)
Je les leur montre. | I show them to them. (2, 3)
Il m'en parle. | He talks to me about it. (1, 5)
Je lui en donne. | I give him some. (3, 5)
Il y en a. | There are some. (4, 5)
```

Two limits keep the table simple. First, *me, te, nous, vous* do not combine with *lui* or *leur*: a sentence such as *il me lui donne* does not exist. Second, the combination *y* + *en* only occurs in *il y en a* and a few similar sentences.

### With a negative, an infinitive and the passé composé

The group of pronouns stays together and always goes in the same place.

| Situation | Where the pronouns go | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Negative | between *ne* and the verb | *Je ne le lui ai pas dit.* |
| Infinitive | before the infinitive | *Je vais te le dire.* |
| Passé composé | before the auxiliary | *Je la lui ai envoyée.* |
| Two verbs | before the second verb | *Tu peux me l'envoyer?* |

In the passé composé, the participle agrees with the direct object (*le, la, l', les*) if it stands before the verb: *la facture, je la lui ai envoyée*; *les clés, je les lui ai remises*. It does not agree with *lui*, *leur*, *me* or *te* when these are indirect objects.

### The imperative

In the affirmative imperative, the pronouns come after the verb, joined by hyphens, and the order changes: the direct object comes first.

| Affirmative | English |
|---|---|
| *Donne-le-moi.* | Give it to me. |
| *Envoyez-la-lui.* | Send it to him. |
| *Donnez-m'en.* | Give me some. |
| *Montre-les-nous.* | Show them to us. |
| *Vas-y.* | Go ahead. |

Order after the verb: *le, la, les*, then *moi, toi, lui, nous, vous, leur*, then *y*, then *en*. *Moi* and *toi* become *m'* and *t'* before *en*: *donnez-m'en*. In the negative imperative, the pronouns go back to the normal order, before the verb: *Ne me le donne pas.* *Ne le lui envoyez pas.*

### Verbs for office use

| Verb | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *transmettre* | to forward, to pass on | *Je te le transmets.* |
| *remettre* | to hand over, to put back | *Elle me les a remis.* |
| *renvoyer* | to send back | *Je vous le renvoie.* |
| *livrer* | to deliver | *On vous les livre demain.* |
| *rembourser* | to repay | *Je le leur rembourse.* |
| *confier* | to entrust | *Il me l'a confié.* |
| *fournir* | to provide | *Nous vous en fournissons.* |

## Écoutez

Écoutez le dialogue. Deux collègues, Inès et Paul, travaillent dans un bureau. Ils se disent *tu*. Inès demande à Paul de transmettre des documents. Listen for the two small words before each verb and note their order.

```fr
Inès: Paul, tu as reçu la facture du fournisseur? | Paul, did you receive the supplier's invoice?
Paul: Oui, je l'ai reçue ce matin. | Yes, I received it this morning.
Inès: Tu peux me la transmettre? | Can you forward it to me?
Paul: Je te la transmets tout de suite. | I'll forward it to you right away.
Inès: Et la facture pour le client? Tu la lui as envoyée? | And the invoice for the client? Did you send it to him?
Paul: Non, je ne la lui ai pas encore envoyée. Je vais la lui envoyer cet après-midi. | No, I haven't sent it to him yet. I'll send it to him this afternoon.
Inès: Parfait. Et les photocopies? | Perfect. And the photocopies?
Paul: Je te les ai laissées sur ton bureau. Si tu veux des copies, je t'en fais. | I left them on your desk for you. If you want copies, I'll make you some.
Inès: Montre-les-moi demain. Et rends-lui le dossier. | Show them to me tomorrow. And give him back the file.
```

## À vous

1. Remplacez les noms par des pronoms : *Je donne le livre à Marie.* *Il envoie les documents au client.* *Nous montrons les photos à nos amis.*
2. Mettez à la forme négative : *Je te le dis.* *Elle me les a donnés.*
3. Mettez à l'impératif affirmatif : *Tu me donnes la clé.* *Vous nous envoyez le rapport.* *Tu nous en donnes.*
4. Corrigez les deux phrases du bloc.

```fr
Je lui le donne. {x}
Je ne le lui ai envoyé pas. {x}
```

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. *Je le lui donne.* *Il les lui envoie.* *Nous les leur montrons.*
2. *Je ne te le dis pas.* *Elle ne me les a pas donnés.*
3. *Donne-la-moi.* *Envoyez-le-nous.* *Donne-nous-en.* (*nous* comes before *en*.)
4. *Je le lui donne.* (*le* comes before *lui*.) *Je ne le lui ai pas envoyé.* (*ne ... pas* goes around the auxiliary.)

</details>

## Dites-le

Dites les phrases à voix haute.

```fr
Je te le donne. | I give it to you.
Je le lui ai envoyé hier. | I sent it to him yesterday.
Tu peux me l'envoyer? | Can you send it to me?
Donne-le-moi. | Give it to me.
```

## Erreurs fréquentes

Putting *lui* before *le*.

```fr
Je lui le donne. {x}
```

Say *je le lui donne*. The direct object (*le, la, les*) comes first when *lui* or *leur* is the second pronoun.

Using the imperative order in the negative.

```fr
Ne donne-le-moi pas. {x}
```

In the negative, say *ne me le donne pas*.

Forgetting the agreement in the passé composé.

```fr
La facture, je la lui ai envoyé. {x}
```

Say *envoyée*.

## Termes clés

- **Pronoun order**: the fixed order of two object pronouns before a verb: *me/te/nous/vous*, then *le/la/les*, then *lui/leur*, then *y*, then *en*.
- **Direct object**: the noun or pronoun that receives the action without a preposition (*le dossier* in *je donne le dossier*).
- **Indirect object**: the person introduced by *à* (*à Marc* in *je donne le dossier à Marc*).
