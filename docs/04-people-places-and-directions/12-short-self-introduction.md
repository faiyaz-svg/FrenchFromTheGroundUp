# A short self-introduction

<span class="tag tag-write">Write</span> <span class="tag tag-a1">A1</span>

After this lesson you can write a 50 to 70 word self-introduction for a language-exchange site: your name, age, city, work or study, family, likes and languages.

Before this: [Être and introducing yourself](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/10-etre-and-introductions.md), [Avoir and il y a](../02-sounds-spelling-and-everyday-things/07-avoir-and-il-y-a.md), [Regular -er verbs](../03-verbs-and-questions/02-regular-er-verbs.md), [Possessives and family](03-possessives-and-family.md), [Adjectives and agreement](01-adjectives-agreement.md). You need: a keyboard that types accents, or paper.

## The idea

A language-exchange site is a place where people who want to practise French or English find each other. A good profile is short and clear. It says who you are, and it says what you can talk about. You already have all the grammar. This lesson is about choosing sentences and writing them without mistakes.

### A model

Read it, then press play.

```fr
Salut! Je m'appelle Nina. J'ai vingt-huit ans et j'habite à Gatineau. | Hi! My name is Nina. I'm twenty-eight and I live in Gatineau.
Je suis comptable et je travaille dans un grand bureau. | I'm an accountant and I work in a big office.
J'ai un frère et une petite sœur. | I have a brother and a little sister.
J'aime la musique, la lecture et les films. | I like music, reading and films.
Je parle anglais et un peu français. Je ne parle pas espagnol. | I speak English and a little French. I don't speak Spanish.
Je cherche des amis pour parler français. | I'm looking for friends to speak French.
```

That is 55 words, with ten conjugated verbs, two adjectives (*grand* and *petite*) and one negative sentence.

### A template

Copy this and fill in the blanks. Keep each answer short.

| Part | Sentence pattern |
|---|---|
| Name | *Je m'appelle ___.* |
| Age, city | *J'ai ___ ans et j'habite à ___.* |
| Work or study | *Je suis ___ et je travaille ... / J'étudie ...* |
| Family | *J'ai ___.* |
| Likes | *J'aime ___, ___ et ___.* |
| Languages | *Je parle ___.* |
| A negative | *Je ne ___ pas ___.* |

### Three small rules for this text

1. After *aimer*, *adorer* and *détester*, the noun has an article: *J'aime la musique*. After *parler* with a language, the article is usually left out: *Je parle anglais*. *Je parle l'anglais* is also correct, but the short form is more common. For liking a language, keep the article: *J'aime l'anglais*.
2. Languages, and nationalities used as adjectives, are written with a small letter in French: *anglais*, *espagnol*, *français*, *canadienne*.
3. After a negative, *un*, *une* and *des* become *de*: *Je n'ai pas de frère*. In writing, keep both parts of the negative: *Je ne parle pas espagnol*.

### Check it

Read your text once for each of these questions: does each verb match its subject, does each adjective match its noun, does each noun have its article, and does every accent appear? If accents are a problem, see [Typing accents on your keyboard](../01-first-sounds-and-greetings/02-typing-accents.md).

## Try it

Four lines below have an error. Find it and rewrite the line correctly. The lines marked as errors have no sound button.

```fr
J'aime musique. {x}
Je habite à Gatineau. {x}
Je n'ai pas un frère. {x}
Elle est une infirmière. {x}
```

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. *J'aime la musique.* After *aimer*, the noun needs an article.
2. *J'habite à Gatineau.* Before a vowel sound, *je* becomes *j'*.
3. *Je n'ai pas de frère.* After the negative, *un* becomes *de*.
4. *Elle est infirmière.* A job has no article after *être*.

</details>

## Say it

Read your finished profile aloud. These lines are a starting point.

```fr
Je m'appelle Nina et j'habite à Gatineau. | My name is Nina and I live in Gatineau.
Je suis comptable. | I'm an accountant.
J'aime la musique et la lecture. | I like music and reading.
Je parle anglais et un peu français. | I speak English and a little French.
Je ne parle pas espagnol. | I don't speak Spanish.
```

## The writing task

Write a 50 to 70 word profile for a language-exchange site. Your text must have:

- at least six verbs,
- at least two adjectives,
- at least one negative sentence.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>A sample answer</summary>

*Bonjour! Je m'appelle Omar. J'ai trente ans et j'habite à Toronto. Je suis ingénieur et je travaille au centre-ville. J'ai une grande famille : deux frères et une sœur. Ma sœur est très sympa. J'aime le sport, la musique et la lecture. Je parle anglais et un peu français. Je n'étudie pas l'espagnol. Je cherche des amis pour parler français.*

This sample has 59 words, eleven conjugated verbs, two adjectives (*grande* and *sympa*) and one negative. Check your own text against this list.

</details>

## Common mistakes

Writing *J'aime musique* with no article. After *aimer*, the noun has one.

Writing *Je habite*. Before a vowel sound, *je* becomes *j'*: *J'habite*.

Writing *Je n'ai pas des frères*. After the negative, use *de*: *Je n'ai pas de frères*.
