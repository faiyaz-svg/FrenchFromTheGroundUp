## Cheat sheet

Grammar tables for this stage.

**Adjective endings** (masculine, feminine). Plural: add *-s*, no change after *-s* or *-x*, *-al* becomes *-aux*.

| Masculine | Feminine | Example |
|---|---|---|
| (basic) | add *-e* | *petit*, *petite* |
| *-e* | same | *jeune*, *triste* |
| *-eux* | *-euse* | *heureux*, *heureuse* |
| *-if* | *-ive* | *actif*, *active* |
| *-er* | *-ère* | *cher*, *chère* |
| *-on*, *-el*, *-il* | *-onne*, *-elle*, *-ille* | *bon*, *naturel*, *gentil* |
| *beau*, *nouveau*, *vieux* | *belle*, *nouvelle*, *vieille* | *bel*, *nouvel*, *vieil* before a vowel |

Position: most adjectives and all colours go after the noun. BAGS (beauty, age, goodness, size) go before: *beau*, *joli*, *jeune*, *vieux*, *nouveau*, *bon*, *mauvais*, *grand*, *petit*, *gros*. *Orange* and *marron* never change.

**Possessives and demonstratives**

| | masculine | feminine | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| my, your, his or her | *mon*, *ton*, *son* | *ma*, *ta*, *sa* | *mes*, *tes*, *ses* |
| our, your, their | *notre*, *votre*, *leur* | *notre*, *votre*, *leur* | *nos*, *vos*, *leurs* |
| this, that | *ce* (*cet* before a vowel) | *cette* | *ces* |

Before a feminine noun that starts with a vowel: *mon amie*. Stressed pronouns: *moi*, *toi*, *lui*, *elle*, *nous*, *vous*, *eux*, *elles*.

**Places.** *de* + *le* = *du*, *de* + *les* = *des*. Cities: *à Montréal*. Masculine countries and provinces: *au Canada*, *au Québec* (the province). Feminine or vowel: *en France*, *en Ontario*. Plural: *aux États-Unis*. The city is *à Québec*.

**Futur proche.** *aller* + infinitive: *je vais manger*, *je ne vais pas manger*.

**C'est or il est.** *C'est* + article, name, stressed pronoun, or a general opinion: *C'est un médecin*, *C'est facile*. *Il est* or *elle est* + adjective or job without article: *Il est médecin*.

Key phrases.

```fr
Elle est jeune et sérieuse. | She is young and serious.
J'ai un frère et une sœur. | I have a brother and a sister.
Moi, j'aime cette chaise. | I like this chair.
La banque est en face du parc. | The bank is across from the park.
Je vais travailler demain. | I'm going to work tomorrow.
Il est médecin. C'est un bon médecin. | He is a doctor. He is a good doctor.
Pour aller à la pharmacie, s'il vous plaît? | How do I get to the pharmacy, please?
Continuez tout droit, tournez à gauche. | Keep going straight, turn left.
```

Sounds. A final consonant is silent in the masculine and heard in the feminine: *petit* /pəti/, *petite* /pətit/. In Quebec, *t* and *d* before /i/ and /y/ get a hiss: *tu* [t͡sy]. The vowels /i y u/ are loose in closed syllables: *jupe* [ʒʏp], *soupe* [sʊp].
