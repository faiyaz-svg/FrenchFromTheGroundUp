# The futur proche

## The idea

The **futur proche** (the "near future") is the easiest way to talk about what will happen. You use the present of *aller* plus a verb in its **infinitive**, the "to ..." form listed in the dictionary: *parler*, *manger*, *travailler*. In English you do the same thing: "I am going to eat".

### Building it

| Pronoun | *aller* | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *je* | *vais* | *Je vais manger.* |
| *tu* | *vas* | *Tu vas travailler.* |
| *il / elle / on* | *va* | *Elle va parler.* |
| *nous* | *allons* | *Nous allons regarder un film.* |
| *vous* | *allez* | *Vous allez habiter à Ottawa.* |
| *ils / elles* | *vont* | *Ils vont jouer.* |

*Aller* changes with the subject, and the second verb never changes. It stays in the infinitive. Be careful not to mix this up with *aller à* and a place: *Je vais à Montréal* means "I am going to Montreal", and *Je vais manger* means "I am going to eat".

*On* often means "we" in speech, with the verb form of *il*: *On va manger*.

### Negative and questions

Put *ne ... pas* around *aller*, and leave the infinitive alone: *Je ne vais pas manger*. *Nous n'allons pas travailler demain*.

For questions, use rising intonation or *est-ce que*: *Tu vas travailler demain?*, *Est-ce que vous allez habiter à Ottawa?*. Question words come first: *Qu'est-ce que tu vas faire?* ("What are you going to do?"), *Où est-ce que tu vas manger?* ("Where are you going to eat?").

### Time words

| French | English |
|---|---|
| *tout de suite* | right away |
| *bientôt* | soon |
| *ce matin*, *cet après-midi*, *ce soir* | this morning, this afternoon, tonight |
| *demain*, *après-demain* | tomorrow, the day after tomorrow |
| *la semaine prochaine* | next week |
| *le mois prochain* | next month |
| *dans une heure*, *dans trois jours* | in an hour, in three days |

*Cet après-midi* takes *cet* because *après-midi* is usually masculine and starts with a vowel sound (see [This, that, and moi, toi](04-this-that-and-moi-toi.md)). *Dans* plus a time means "in that much time from now": *dans une heure* is one hour from now. It does not mean "within an hour".

## Hear it

Louis and Chantal, a couple in Gatineau (*tu*), plan a move. Then Adam phones Nina at 8 a.m. to ask about her day (*tu*). Listen for *je vais* and *on va*, and for the infinitive that follows.

```fr
Chantal: Alors, on va vraiment déménager? | So, are we really going to move?
Louis: Oui! Ce soir, je vais chercher un appartement près du travail. | Yes! Tonight, I'm going to look for an apartment near work.
Chantal: Moi, je vais regarder les photos demain matin. | I'm going to look at the photos tomorrow morning.
Louis: Samedi, on va regarder trois appartements. | On Saturday, we're going to look at three apartments.
Chantal: Et après, on va manger au restaurant? | And after, are we going to eat at a restaurant?
Louis: Bien sûr! Je ne vais pas cuisiner, je suis trop fatigué. | Of course! I'm not going to cook, I'm too tired.

Adam: Allô Nina, c'est Adam. Qu'est-ce que tu vas faire aujourd'hui? | Hi Nina, it's Adam. What are you going to do today?
Nina: Ce matin, je vais travailler. Cet après-midi, je vais chez le docteur, à 15 h 30. | This morning, I'm going to work. This afternoon, I'm going to the doctor, at 3:30 p.m.
Adam: Et ce soir? | And tonight?
Nina: Je vais rester à la maison. | I'm going to stay at home.
Adam: Alors, on va au cinéma après-demain? | Then are we going to the movies the day after tomorrow?
Nina: Oui! Après-demain, je suis libre. | Yes! The day after tomorrow, I'm free.
```

## Try it

1. Put into the futur proche: *Je mange.* *Nous travaillons.* *Elles parlent.* *Tu regardes un film.*
2. Make these negative: *Je vais cuisiner.* *Ils vont habiter ici.*
3. Say in French: "We are going to study tomorrow." "Are you (*vous*) going to stay in Gatineau next month?"
4. Say what you are going to do tonight, tomorrow and next week, in three sentences.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. *Je vais manger.* *Nous allons travailler.* *Elles vont parler.* *Tu vas regarder un film.*
2. *Je ne vais pas cuisiner.* *Ils ne vont pas habiter ici.*
3. *Nous allons étudier demain.* *Vous allez rester à Gatineau le mois prochain?*
4. Example: *Ce soir, je vais regarder un film. Demain, je vais travailler. La semaine prochaine, je vais voyager.* Each sentence needs a form of *aller* and an infinitive.

</details>

## Say it

```fr
Je vais manger tout de suite. | I'm going to eat right away.
Tu vas travailler demain? | Are you going to work tomorrow?
On va regarder un film ce soir. | We're going to watch a film tonight.
Nous n'allons pas cuisiner. | We're not going to cook.
Ils vont jouer bientôt. | They're going to play soon.
```

## Common mistakes

Changing the second verb. Say *je vais manger*, not *je vais mange*.

Putting *pas* after the infinitive. *Pas* goes after *vais*: *Je ne vais pas manger*.

Reading *dans une heure* as "within an hour". It means "one hour from now".

## Key terms

- **Infinitive**: the form of a verb found in the dictionary, such as *parler* ("to speak").
- **Futur proche**: the near future, made with *aller* and an infinitive.
