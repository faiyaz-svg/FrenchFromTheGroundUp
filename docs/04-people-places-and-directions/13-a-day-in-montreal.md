# A day in Montreal

<span class="tag tag-listen">Listen</span> <span class="tag tag-review">Review</span> <span class="tag tag-a1">A1</span>

After this lesson you can follow a three-minute talk about a day in a city, and say what you liked about it, using everything from this stage.

Before this: every lesson in stage 4, especially [The futur proche](06-futur-proche.md), [Prepositions of place](05-prepositions-of-place.md) and [Following directions](11-following-directions.md). You need: paper and a pencil.

## The idea

This lesson has no new grammar. It is one longer text to listen to, and it brings back the stage: adjectives, possessives, *ce* and *cette*, place words, the *futur proche*, *c'est* and directions. Listen three times.

1. Listen with the translation hidden. Write the places you hear.
2. Listen again. Write what happens at each place.
3. Read the text and check.

The places are real, and the plan is made up. The text gives no opening times. For those, use the official site of each place.

## Hear it

A guide speaks to a group of visitors (*vous*) at the start of a day in Montréal.

```fr
Guide: Bonjour à tous! Aujourd'hui, nous allons marcher dans Montréal. | Hello everyone! Today, we are going to walk in Montréal.
Guide: Ce matin, nous allons au Vieux-Port. Il y a un grand parc près du fleuve. C'est magnifique! | This morning, we are going to the Old Port. There is a big park near the river. It's magnificent!
Guide: Après, nous allons manger. Il y a un petit restaurant en face de la place. Les gâteaux sont délicieux. | After that, we are going to eat. There is a small restaurant across from the square. The cakes are delicious.

Guide: Cet après-midi, nous allons au marché Jean-Talon. Il y a des fruits, des fleurs et des gâteaux. Vous allez aimer ce marché! | This afternoon, we are going to the Jean-Talon market. There are fruits, flowers and cakes. You are going to like this market!
Guide: Nous allons voyager en métro. C'est facile, et il y a une correspondance. Regardez la carte : nous sommes ici. | We are going to travel by métro. It's easy, and there is a transfer. Look at the map: we are here.

Guide: Ce soir, nous allons marcher sur la montagne. Il y a un sentier. La vue est magnifique, mais c'est un peu long. | Tonight, we are going to walk up the mountain. There is a trail. The view is magnificent, but it's a bit long.
Guide: Voilà notre visite. Bonne journée à tous! | That is our visit. Have a good day, everyone!
```

Now two of the visitors (*tu*) talk about the plan.

```fr
Amina: Moi, j'aime le marché. Et toi? | I like the market. And you?
Karim: Moi aussi. Mais je ne vais pas marcher sur la montagne, je suis fatigué! | Me too. But I'm not going to walk up the mountain, I'm tired!
Amina: Ce n'est pas difficile. C'est une belle vue, tu vas adorer! | It's not hard. It's a nice view, you're going to love it!
Karim: D'accord, je vais regarder la vue. | OK, I'm going to look at the view.
```

## Try it

1. Which three places does the guide name? Put them in order.
2. What is at the market, and how do the visitors travel?
3. What is at the end of the day, and what is "a bit long"?
4. In the second dialogue, who is tired, and what does the other person say about the view?
5. Say what you are going to do tomorrow in two sentences, using the *futur proche* and a place word.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. The Vieux-Port (morning), the marché Jean-Talon (afternoon), and the mountain (evening).
2. Fruits, flowers and cakes (*des fruits, des fleurs et des gâteaux*). They travel by métro.
3. A walk on the mountain, and the trail is a bit long (*c'est un peu long*).
4. Karim is tired. Amina says it is a nice view.
5. Example: *Demain, je vais travailler. Après, je vais manger au restaurant en face du parc.*

</details>

## Say it

```fr
Aujourd'hui, nous allons marcher dans Montréal. | Today, we are going to walk in Montréal.
Il y a un grand parc près du fleuve. | There is a big park near the river.
Ce marché est magnifique. | This market is magnificent.
Nous allons voyager en métro. | We are going to travel by métro.
La vue est belle. | The view is beautiful.
```

## Common mistakes

Trying to catch every word in the guide's talk. Catch the places and the verbs first.

Missing *nous allons* at the start of each sentence. It tells you what will happen next.

Mixing up *c'est* and *il est* in your own description. Use *c'est* for a general opinion and *il est* for a particular thing.
