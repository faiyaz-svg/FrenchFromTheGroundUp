# Irregular past participles

<span class="tag tag-grammar">Grammar</span> <span class="tag tag-a2">A2</span>

After this lesson you can use the past participles of *avoir*, *être*, *faire*, *prendre*, *voir* and *boire* in the passé composé, and you can use the new verbs *mettre*, *ouvrir*, *offrir*, *fermer* and *gagner* in the present and the past.

Before this: [The passé composé with avoir](03-passe-compose-avoir.md), [Prendre, venir, boire and voir](02-prendre-venir-boire-voir.md), [Aller and faire](../03-verbs-and-questions/05-aller-and-faire.md). You need: nothing.

## The idea

In the last lesson, the participle was easy to build: *-é*, *-i* or *-u* in place of the ending. Some verbs do not follow that rule. Their participle is **irregular**, which means you learn it with the verb. The good news is that the list is short for the verbs you already know, and the participles fall into a few groups of sound. This lesson covers only verbs you have already met. Participles of verbs that come later, such as *vouloir* or *pouvoir*, are taught with those verbs.

The helper stays *avoir*: *j'ai eu*, *tu as vu*, *il a pris*. Only the participle is different.

### Participles you must know

| Verb | Participle | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *avoir* | *eu* | /y/ | *J'ai eu peur.* |
| *être* | *été* | /ete/ | *Il a été fatigué.* |
| *faire* | *fait* | /fɛ/ | *Nous avons fait le ménage.* |
| *prendre* | *pris* | /pʁi/ | *Elle a pris l'autobus.* |
| *comprendre* | *compris* | /kɔ̃pʁi/ | *Tu as compris?* |
| *apprendre* | *appris* | /apʁi/ | *J'ai appris le français.* |
| *voir* | *vu* | /vy/ | *Ils ont vu un film.* |
| *boire* | *bu* | /by/ | *J'ai bu un thé.* |

*Eu* is the one that trips people: the spelling *eu* is the sound /y/, the same vowel as in *tu* and *vu*, and not the sound of *peu*. So *j'ai eu* is /ʒe y/. *Été* /ete/ is the participle of *être*: it is the same as the French word for "summer", and the context tells you which one you mean. *Fait* ends in a silent *t*, so you say /fɛ/. The *-prendre* verbs share *pris*: *pris*, *compris*, *appris*.

### Three new verbs: mettre, ouvrir, offrir

*Mettre* means "to put" and "to put on" (clothes). *Ouvrir* means "to open" and *offrir* means "to give as a gift, to offer". Their present forms are not the same type.

| Subject | *mettre* | *ouvrir* | *offrir* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *je* | *mets* | *ouvre* | *offre* |
| *tu* | *mets* | *ouvres* | *offres* |
| *il / elle / on* | *met* | *ouvre* | *offre* |
| *nous* | *mettons* | *ouvrons* | *offrons* |
| *vous* | *mettez* | *ouvrez* | *offrez* |
| *ils / elles* | *mettent* | *ouvrent* | *offrent* |

*Ouvrir* and *offrir* end in *-ir* but use the endings of an -er verb: `e, es, e, ons, ez, ent`. Do not use the endings of *finir*. Their participles are *ouvert* /uvɛʁ/ and *offert* /ɔfɛʁ/. The *t* is pronounced, which makes them easy to spot by ear. *Mettre* has the participle *mis* /mi/.

*Il a mis son manteau.* "He put on his coat."
*Elle a ouvert la porte.* "She opened the door."
*Il a offert un gâteau à sa mère.* "He gave his mother a cake."

### Two new regular verbs

*Fermer* (to close) and *gagner* (to win, to earn) are regular -er verbs, so their participles are *fermé* and *gagné*. *Ouvrir* and *fermer* are opposites, and you will hear both when a shop opens or closes.

### A way to learn them

Group the participles by their ending:

| Ending | Participles |
|---|---|
| *-u* | *eu*, *vu*, *bu* |
| *-is* | *pris*, *compris*, *appris*, *mis* |
| *-ert* | *ouvert*, *offert* |
| other | *été*, *fait* |

Say each group aloud, with *j'ai* in front. A group of three or four sounds is much easier to hold than eight separate words.

## Hear it

A customer goes to the lost-property counter of a bus company in Montréal. She and the employee use *vous*. Listen for the participles *pris*, *mis*, *vu*, *ouvert* and *eu*, and for the sound of *j'ai* in front of each one.

```fr
Employé: Bonjour, madame. Vous cherchez un objet perdu? | Hello, madam. Are you looking for a lost object?
Cliente: Oui, j'ai perdu mon sac hier soir. J'ai pris l'autobus à six heures. | Yes, I lost my bag last night. I took the bus at six o'clock.
Employé: Il est comment, votre sac? | What does your bag look like?
Cliente: Il est noir et grand. J'ai mis mon sac près de la porte. | It's black and big. I put my bag near the door.
Employé: Qu'est-ce que vous avez mis dans le sac? | What did you put in the bag?
Cliente: J'ai mis un livre, une clé, un parapluie et mon téléphone. | I put in a book, a key, an umbrella and my phone.
Employé: Un moment. ... Un chauffeur a trouvé un sac noir ce matin. Il a ouvert le sac et il a vu un livre. | One moment. ... A driver found a black bag this morning. He opened the bag and he saw a book.
Cliente: C'est mon sac! J'ai eu peur. Merci beaucoup. | That's my bag! I was scared. Thank you very much.
```

## Try it

1. Write the participle: *faire*, *prendre*, *voir*, *boire*, *avoir*, *être*, *mettre*, *ouvrir*.
2. Fill in the passé composé: *Nous ___ (prendre) l'autobus.* *Elle ___ (voir) un film.* *Ils ___ (boire) un jus.* *J'___ (avoir) peur.*
3. Say in French: "I put my coat on the chair." "We won the match."
4. Which two participles end in a pronounced *t* and look like *-ir* verbs in the infinitive?
5. Say: *Hier, j'ai fait le ménage et j'ai fermé la porte.* Then write what it means in English.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. *fait*, *pris*, *vu*, *bu*, *eu*, *été*, *mis*, *ouvert*.
2. *Nous avons pris l'autobus.* *Elle a vu un film.* *Ils ont bu un jus.* *J'ai eu peur.*
3. *J'ai mis mon manteau sur la chaise.* *Nous avons gagné le match.*
4. *ouvert* (from *ouvrir*) and *offert* (from *offrir*).
5. "Yesterday I did the housework and closed the door."

</details>

## Say it

```fr
J'ai eu peur. | I was scared.
Nous avons fait le ménage. | We did the housework.
Elle a pris l'autobus. | She took the bus.
Il a mis son manteau. | He put on his coat.
Ils ont ouvert la porte. | They opened the door.
```

## Common mistakes

Saying *eu* like *peu*. In *j'ai eu* the vowel is /y/, as in *tu*.

Writing *j'ai prendu* or *j'ai faisé*. Regular endings do not apply here, so use *pris* and *fait*.

Conjugating *ouvrir* like *finir*: *nous ouvrissons*. The present is *j'ouvre*, *nous ouvrons*, like an -er verb.

## Key terms
