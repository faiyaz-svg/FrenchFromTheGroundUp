## The idea

You already say *je m'appelle*, and in [Making plans](../03-verbs-and-questions/08-making-plans.md) you learned *on se retrouve*. Both are **reflexive verbs** (*les verbes pronominaux*): the action goes back to the person who does it. *Je m'appelle Marc* is literally "I call myself Marc". A reflexive verb has an extra small word, the **reflexive pronoun** (*le pronom réfléchi*), between the subject and the verb. In the infinitive it appears as *se*: *se lever*, "to get up".

English often has no such word ("I get up", "I wash"), so you must remember the pronoun every time.

### The pronouns

| Subject | Reflexive pronoun | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *je* | *me* (*m'*) | *je me couche* |
| *tu* | *te* (*t'*) | *tu te couches* |
| *il / elle / on* | *se* (*s'*) | *il se couche* |
| *nous* | *nous* | *nous nous couchons* |
| *vous* | *vous* | *vous vous couchez* |
| *ils / elles* | *se* (*s'*) | *ils se couchent* |

The pronouns shorten to *m'*, *t'* and *s'* before a vowel or a silent *h*: *je m'habille*, *tu t'amuses*, *il s'occupe*. With *nous nous* and *vous vous*, the same word twice is correct. Before a vowel there is a liaison: *nous nous amusons* /nu nuz amyzɔ̃/.

### Daily routine verbs

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *se réveiller* | to wake up |
| *se lever* | to get up |
| *se laver* | to wash |
| *s'habiller* | to get dressed |
| *se coucher* | to go to bed |
| *se dépêcher* | to hurry |
| *se reposer* | to rest |
| *se promener* | to go for a walk |
| *s'amuser* | to have fun |
| *s'occuper de* | to take care of, to deal with |
| *se retrouver* | to meet (each other), to meet up |
| *se trouver* | to be located |

*Se lever* and *se promener* have the spelling change you saw in *acheter*: *je me lève*, *tu te lèves*, *il se lève*, *ils se lèvent*, but *nous nous levons*. With *s'occuper*, the *de* + *le* joins to *du*: *Je m'occupe du café.* *Se trouver* usually describes where a place is: *La gare se trouve près du parc.*

### Negative, question and infinitive

The negative goes around the pronoun and the verb: *Je ne me lève pas tôt.* *Elle ne se dépêche pas.* A question can use a question word and the rising voice: *Tu te lèves à quelle heure?* *Vous vous reposez?*

After *aller* (and after *vouloir*, *pouvoir* and *devoir*), the verb stays in the infinitive, and the pronoun still matches the subject: *Je vais me coucher. Tu vas te reposer. Nous voulons nous promener.* Do not drop the pronoun.

### In the passé composé

All reflexive verbs take *être* in the passé composé: the pronoun comes before *être*, then the participle. *Je me suis levé.* *Tu t'es couchée.* *Elles se sont retrouvées.* In the negative, *ne* and *pas* go around *me suis*: *Je ne me suis pas reposé.*

As with other *être* verbs, the participle usually agrees with the subject: *-e* for a woman, *-s* for several people. A man writes *je me suis levé* and a woman writes *je me suis levée*. They sound the same. This lesson keeps to the common case. The full rule, with its exceptions, comes later in the book.

## Hear it

Listen for the pronoun before each verb, and for the *est* /ɛ/ in *s'est* and *t'es*. Karim works nights as a nurse in Mississauga. He talks to a coworker, and they use *tu*.

```fr
Léa: Karim, tu te couches à quelle heure? | Karim, what time do you go to bed?
Karim: Je me couche à 9 h du matin. | I go to bed at 9 a.m.
Léa: Et tu te réveilles à quelle heure? | And what time do you wake up?
Karim: Je mets mon réveil à 4 h de l'après-midi. Je me lève, je prends une douche, je m'habille. | I set my alarm for 4 p.m. I get up, I take a shower, I get dressed.
Léa: Et le soir, on se retrouve à l'hôpital! | And in the evening we meet at the hospital!
Karim: Oui, mais je ne me dépêche pas. Le week-end, je me repose et je me promène. | Yes, but I don't hurry. On the weekend, I rest and go for a walk.
Léa: Hier, tu t'es couché à quelle heure? | Yesterday, what time did you go to bed?
Karim: Hier, je me suis couché à 10 h. Je ne me suis pas reposé! | Yesterday I went to bed at 10. I didn't rest!
```

## Try it

1. Fill in the verb: *Nous ___ (se lever) à 7 h.* *Ils ___ (s'amuser) bien.* *Je ___ (se dépêcher).* *Tu ___ (se coucher) tôt.*
2. Make it negative: *Je me lève tôt.* *Elle s'habille.* *Ils se couchent tard.*
3. Fill in the infinitive: *Ce soir, je vais ___ (se coucher) tôt.* *Nous voulons ___ (se promener).*
4. Put into the passé composé: *Elle se réveille à 7 h.* *Nous nous amusons.* *Je ne me repose pas* (a man speaking).
5. Say "What time do you get up?" to a friend.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. *Nous nous levons à 7 h.* *Ils s'amusent bien.* *Je me dépêche.* *Tu te couches tôt.*
2. *Je ne me lève pas tôt.* *Elle ne s'habille pas.* *Ils ne se couchent pas tard.*
3. *Ce soir, je vais me coucher tôt.* *Nous voulons nous promener.*
4. *Elle s'est réveillée à 7 h.* *Nous nous sommes amusés* (or *amusées*, if all are women). *Je ne me suis pas reposé.*
5. *Tu te lèves à quelle heure?*

</details>

## Say it

```fr
Je me lève à 7 h. | I get up at 7.
Tu te couches à quelle heure? | What time do you go to bed?
Nous nous retrouvons au parc. | We meet at the park.
Je vais me coucher tôt. | I'm going to go to bed early.
Hier, je me suis reposé. | Yesterday I rested.
```

## Common mistakes

Leaving out the pronoun: *Je lève à 7 h*. A reflexive verb always keeps *me*, *te* or *se*: *Je me lève à 7 h*.

Using *avoir* in the passé composé: *Je me suis couché*, not *Je m'ai couché*. Reflexive verbs always take *être*.

Dropping the pronoun after *aller*: *Je vais coucher tôt*. Keep it and make it match the subject: *Je vais me coucher tôt*.

## In Quebec

In fast casual speech, *je me* shortens to one sound, as in the line below. This is a casual form, and you will hear it in many places. Write the full form.

```fr
J'me lève à 7 h. {qc} | I get up at 7.
```

## Key terms

- **Reflexive verb**: a verb with a pronoun that points back to the subject, such as *se lever*.
- **Reflexive pronoun**: the word *me*, *te*, *se*, *nous* or *vous* used with a reflexive verb.
